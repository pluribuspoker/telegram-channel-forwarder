# God Expert Rules v2

This file is the versioned specification of the deterministic aggregator. No
model reads it. It is hashed into every `god_rules` row so the algorithm that
produced an opinion is pinned the way a prompt is.

Input: the aggregator input built by `moe_god.build_aggregator_input`.

1. Voices. One approved opinion per enabled non-aggregator expert: the latest
   approved row on the expert's registry `default_model`, otherwise the latest
   approved row on any model. The rule used is recorded per voice.
2. Market. BetOnline latest full-game prices are de-vigged pairwise:
   `fair_a = implied(a) / (implied(a) + implied(b))`.
3. Conversion. Each voice's projected margin and total become cover and over
   probabilities through the policy's `margin_model`. `normal` (the default)
   draws the residual from a normal model with the policy sigmas:
   `p_cover_home = Phi((margin + home_spread) / sigma_margin)` and
   `p_over = Phi((total - line) / sigma_total)`. `empirical` draws it from
   the closing-line bin of `moe/priors/nfl_margins_v1.json` (nflverse
   closes, 2016-2025): `P(r > t) + P(r = t) / 2`, exact on the half-point
   lattice and linear between points, with `t = -(margin + home_spread)`
   for the side and `t = line - total` for the total; a bin under the
   table's `min_games`, or a line outside it, falls back to the normal
   model. The table's path and sha256 ride in the input as `margin_table`.
4. Pool. Weighted mean of the voices' home win probability, expected margin,
   and projected total. Weights are 1.0 until a voice has
   `weights_min_resolved` graded games; then Hedge weights
   `exp(-eta * resolved * (brier - mean_brier))`, clipped to the policy
   bounds, apply to every eligible voice. Two discounts follow, both
   recorded in the feature block:
   - Evidence overlap. Every `W-L` or `W-L-T` record a voice cites in its
     supporting factors or counterarguments becomes a tuple
     `(W, L, T, games)`, the cohort size being the "N games" count nearest
     the record in the same item, else `W+L+T`. Two voices overlap by the
     Jaccard index of their tuple sets. Voices rank by expert id; a voice's
     weight is divided by `1 + (its overlap with every voice ranked before
     it)`, so a table recited twice is not counted twice.
   - Market relevance. Each expert declares the markets it informs
     (registry `markets`, a subset of `side` and `total`; both when
     absent). The side pool — win probability, margin, cover probability,
     winner votes — averages the side-informed voices; the total pool —
     projected total, over probability — averages the total-informed ones.
     An empty pool has nothing to shrink: the blend for that market is the
     market expectation.
5. Shrink. Blend = `lambda * pool + (1 - lambda) * market expectation` for
   the probability (toward the fair moneyline), the margin (toward minus the
   home spread), and the total (toward the line).
6. Coherence. If the blended probability is exactly 0.5 or the margin exactly
   0, the market favorite breaks the tie. If probability and margin disagree
   in sign, the margin is clamped to follow the probability. Either case is
   recorded as a discarded consideration.
7. Policy, shared with the judge. Edge = estimate minus fair for each side of
   the spread and the total. Bet the larger edge when it reaches
   `edge_threshold` and the expected value at the posted price is positive;
   stars by `star_edges`; stake = `kelly_fraction` of full Kelly, capped at
   `max_stake_fraction`. A leg whose own line or price moved against it
   since open by at least `veto_adverse_spread_points`,
   `veto_adverse_total_points`, or `veto_adverse_price_cents` passes as
   `adverse move`; a leg whose expected value per unit is under
   `min_ev_per_unit` passes as `ev floor`. Otherwise PASS with one star.

Parameters live under `aggregator_policy` in `moe/experts.yaml` and are copied
into every input, so a parameter change shows in the input hash.
