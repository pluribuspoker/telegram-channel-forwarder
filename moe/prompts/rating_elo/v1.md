# Rating Expert (Elo) v1

This file is the versioned specification of the deterministic rating voice.
No model reads it. It is hashed into every `rating_elo` row so the algorithm
that produced an opinion is pinned the way a prompt is.

Input: the rating input built by `moe_rating.build_rating_input` from the
committed prior `moe/priors/nfl_elo_v1.json` and this season's finals.

1. Ratings. One Elo rating per team from every regular-season final since
   1999 (`data/nfl_lines_history.csv`, nflverse). Each game moves the home
   team by `K * m * (S - p)` and the away team by the mirror, where `S` is 1,
   0.5, or 0 for a home win, tie, or loss, `p` is the pregame home win
   probability, and `m = ln(|margin| + 1) * 2.2 / (0.001 * winner_gap + 2.2)`
   is the margin-of-victory multiplier (a favorite's blowout counts less; a
   tie moves nothing).
2. Seasons. Between seasons every rating regresses `regression` of the way
   toward `base_rating`. The prior stores the ratings at the end of its
   `through_season`; the voice rates only the season that follows it,
   regressing once and then applying that season's finals that kicked off
   strictly before the game, in kickoff order.
3. Estimate. `gap = home rating - away rating`, `adjusted = gap + hfa`.
   `p(home) = 1 / (1 + 10 ^ (-adjusted / 400))`; `expected home margin =
   adjusted / points_per_elo`. Ratings are used as written to two decimals,
   so the input's numbers reproduce the estimate. An adjusted gap of exactly
   0 leans home (probability 0.5001, margin 0.01).
4. Scores. The projected total is the league scoring rate stored in the
   prior (the mean total of its `through_season`); predicted scores are
   `(total ± margin) / 2` rounded, never tied. The total carries no
   game-specific signal and the opinion says so.
5. Confidence. Stars from the size of the expected margin: 1 star under 3
   points, then 2, 3, 4, 5 stars at 3, 7, 10, and 14 points.
6. Parameters. `K`, `hfa`, and `regression` are fitted by grid search on
   the prior's fit seasons (minimum Brier of the pregame home win
   probability); `points_per_elo` is the least-squares slope of the actual
   margin on the adjusted gap over the same seasons. The check season is
   never fitted; its Elo Brier and the closing moneyline's Brier are stored
   in the prior and quoted in the opinion.

The prior's path, schema version, and SHA-256 ride in every input, so a
refit changes the input hash of every row generated afterwards.
